Matis Brassard-Verrier

What AI can do still amazes me every day. Whether it actually makes people's lives better is the question I keep coming back to.

I studied data science at ETH Zürich and now build AI systems for a living. I chose this field because I think AI will change a lot about how we live, and I'd rather make sense of that change than just ride it. This site is where I think it through, in projects as well as essays, as someone who builds these systems and wants to understand them.
